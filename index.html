يَوْمُ الْجُمُعَة
Yawm Al-Jumu'ah

The Blessings of Jumu'ah

خَيْرُ يَوْمٍ طَلَعَتْ عَلَيْهِ الشَّمْسُ يَوْمُ الْجُمُعَةِ

"The best day on which the sun has risen is Friday. On it Adam was created, on it he was made to enter Paradise, on it he was expelled from it, and the Hour will not be established except on Friday."

Sahih Muslim
✦ Sa'at al-Istijabah
Make Abundant Du'a Between Asr & Maghrib

"Friday has twelve hours. There is one hour during which if a Muslim asks Allah anything, Allah will give it to him, so find it during the last hour after Asr."

⏱ After Asr → Until Maghrib Adhaan

"If a man takes a bath on Friday, cleans himself as much as he can, oils his hair, applies perfume, sets forth for the mosque, does not separate two people to make a seat for himself, performs as many prayers as written for him, remains silent when the Imam speaks — for his sins between that Friday and the following Friday will be forgiven."

Al-Bukhari

أَذْكَارُ الصَّبَاح
Adhkar Al-Sabah

Morning Adhkar

اَللّٰهُمَّ بِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا وَبِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا

"O Allah, by You we reach the morning and by You we reach the evening, by You we live and by You we die, and to You is the resurrection."

Morning Opening Supplication
✦ Best Time
Recite Between Fajr and Sunrise

The morning adhkār should ideally be recited between Fajr and sunrise. If unable to recite them in this time, you may still recite them afterwards.

⏱ After Fajr → Until Sunrise

"Whoever says in the morning and evening: 'Glory be to Allah and with His praise' one hundred times, no one will come on the Day of Resurrection with anything better."

Sahih Muslim
