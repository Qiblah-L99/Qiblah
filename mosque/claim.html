Qiblah Mosque portal

Claim your mosque

Find your mosque, verify an official email address, then Qiblah will either approve access automatically or send the request for review.

1
Search for the mosque already listed on Qiblah.
2
Use a mosque email address, preferably one matching the mosque website or listing email domain.
3
Approved admins can sign in to manage prayer times, profile details, announcements and display settings.
Free email addresses can still be reviewed, but they will not be auto-approved.
