# UK Prayer Spaces Research

Research date: 4 September 2026

## Inclusion standard

Only publish a space when its location and current availability can be supported by an official venue page, an operator page, or a detailed current access guide. Keep third-party directory results as verification leads, not production data.

Recommended access labels:

- `public`: reachable without a ticket, appointment, or institutional membership
- `customer`: available while using the venue or shopping centre
- `passenger-landside`: inside an airport but before security
- `passenger-airside`: requires a boarding pass and security clearance
- `restricted`: staff, student, patient, or event-ticket access only
- `verify`: plausible listing that still needs confirmation

## Rail, hospitals, and universities

- Birmingham New Street and Glasgow Central have documented multi-faith rooms. Confirm opening hours and whether passengers can enter without a ticket before publishing. Sources: [Birmingham New Street access guide](https://www.accessable.co.uk/birmingham-city-council/access-guides/birmingham-new-street-station), [Glasgow Central station map](https://www.networkrail.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Glasgow-Central-Station-map.pdf).
- Hospitals can provide excellent 24-hour prayer access, but entries must say `patient/visitor access`; they should not imply general walk-in public access. A confirmed example is the Royal Surrey County Hospital chapel and multi-faith room, open 24 hours on Level B of the West Wing: [AccessAble guide](https://www.accessable.co.uk/royal-surrey-nhs-foundation-trust/royal-surrey-county-hospital/access-guides/the-chapel-and-multi-faith-room).
- University rooms should default to `restricted` until the institution explicitly confirms public access. Many are only for students, staff, or contracted personnel.

## Data changes needed before a national import

Each Prayer Space record should carry:

- `access_type`
- `access_notes`
- `security_side` for airports: `landside`, `airside`, or `both`
- `direction` and `cross_carriageway_access` for motorway services
- `opening_hours`
- `wudu`
- `women_space`
- `verified_url`
- `last_verified_at`
- `verification_status`: `confirmed`, `needs_review`, or `temporarily_closed`

The UI should show access restrictions before directions are opened. A user should never drive to a room and only then discover that it requires a boarding pass, student card, hospital visit, or advance request.
