Mosque Admin Terms

These terms apply to mosque representatives who register, claim, or manage a mosque on Qiblah.

Authorisation

You must be authorised by the mosque, Islamic centre, prayer space, trustees, committee, or management team to register or manage the mosque on Qiblah.

Accurate information

You agree to provide accurate mosque details and keep prayer times, contact details, facilities, donation links and announcements up to date.

Review and access

Qiblah may review, approve, reject, suspend or remove mosque admin access if details cannot be verified or access appears unauthorised.

Donation links

Qiblah may display a link to the mosque's own donation platform. Qiblah does not process, hold, refund, or manage donation funds.

Contact

Questions can be sent to info@qiblah.co.uk.
