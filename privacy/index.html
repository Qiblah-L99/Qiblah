Privacy Policy

Qiblah collects the minimum information needed to review mosque registrations, manage mosque admin access, and operate mosque listings.

Information we collect

  • Mosque details such as name, address, area, website and contact details.
  • Admin contact details such as name, email and phone number.
  • Verification notes provided by the mosque representative.

How we use it

We use this information to review registrations, contact mosque representatives, approve or reject admin access, and maintain accurate mosque information on Qiblah.

Donations

Qiblah does not process or hold donations. If a mosque adds a donation link, it points to the mosque's own chosen donation platform.

Retention

Rejected or inactive registration requests should be deleted when no longer needed. Qiblah aims to keep registration review data only for as long as necessary.

Contact

For privacy requests, email info@qiblah.co.uk.
